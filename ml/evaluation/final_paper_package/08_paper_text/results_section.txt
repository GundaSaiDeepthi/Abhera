4. EXPERIMENTAL RESULTS
======================

4.1 Dataset & Evaluation Protocol
The ABHERA system was evaluated using a strict held-out partition split of 1,086 incident narratives:
Training (760), Validation (162), and Held-Out Test (164). Rigorous dataset leakage audits confirmed zero
exact narrative text overlap and zero ID overlap across splits. Decision thresholds were optimized exclusively
on validation data and frozen prior to held-out test evaluation.

4.2 Multi-Label Incident Classification (BERT)
Evaluating the fine-tuned BERT classifier on the 164 held-out test incidents under validation-derived per-label
thresholds (DV=0.65, SH=0.60, ST=0.64, CA=0.72, WH=0.54, OV=0.78) yielded an Exact Match Subset Accuracy of 83.54%
(137/164 cases) and an Elementwise Binary Accuracy of 96.95%. The model achieved Micro Precision of 95.76%,
Micro Recall of 87.29%, and a Micro F1 Score of 91.33% (Macro F1 = 90.37%). Partial Match Success (predicting
at least one ground-truth label) reached 94.51% (155/164 cases).

4.3 Named Entity Recognition (BERT-NER)
Evaluation on 60 held-out gold NER records (66 entity spans) demonstrated exact span & label Micro Precision
of 100.00%, Micro Recall of 98.48%, and Micro F1 of 99.24% (Macro F1 active = 99.00%). Individual active class F1
scores were: PERP_REL (100.0%), LOCATION (100.0%), TIME_FREQ (100.0%), and PLATFORM (97.44%). Classes EVIDENCE
and LAW_SEC had zero test support and were excluded from active class performance averaging.

4.4 Category Legal Retrieval & Anti-Hallucination Safeguards
Category-conditioned legal retrieval across 164 test queries achieved Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR) of 0.9400,
Hit Rate@1 of 93.90%, and Recall@10 of 90.37%. Anti-hallucination database gating achieved a 100.00% Legal Grounding
Success Rate (all returned provisions verified verbatim against laws.csv) and a 100.00% Anti-Hallucination Pass Rate.
Controlled negative testing verified that unmapped queries reliably trigger the exact fallback response:
'Information not available in the provided knowledge base.'

4.5 End-to-End Operational Performance
The fully grounded pipeline success rate—measuring cases satisfying partial classification success, 100% legal grounding,
anti-hallucination pass, and valid report execution—reached 94.51% (155/164 cases). Failure analysis identified exactly
9 classification error cases (7 false-negative boundary cases and 2 category confusion errors between OV and DV).
